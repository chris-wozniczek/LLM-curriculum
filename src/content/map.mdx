## How this curriculum works

This site teaches LLMs by opening real models and reading what is actually inside them. When a lesson says "Qwen3-0.6B has 28 layers", that number was read from the model's own `config.json`, not copied from a blog.

Three kinds of numbers appear, and they are always marked:

<div class="callout measured"><b>Measured</b><p>Produced by a script in this repo's <code>labs/</code> folder, on a real model. You can re-run it and get the same result.</p></div>
<div class="callout source"><b>Source</b><p>Taken from a paper, official documentation, a model card or a config file, with a link.</p></div>
<div class="callout plain"><b>Illustration</b><p>A made-up example to explain an idea (for instance, round numbers in a diagram). Never presented as a fact about a real model.</p></div>

## Reading order

You don't have to go 00 → 16. Two good routes:

- **"I want to understand the ViC305-style posts"**: 05 (repo files) → 06 (number formats) → 07 (quantization, including top-1 and KL divergence) → 13 (recipes) → 08 (experts). These are the modules ready now.
- **"I want the whole picture"**: 01 → 02 → 03 → 04, then 05 → 09, then 10 → 12, then 14 → 16.

## Finding things

- **Search** (press <code>/</code> or <code>Ctrl/⌘ K</code>) covers every lesson section and the glossary.
- **Dotted words** like <T k="bpw">bpw</T> or <T k="kld">KL divergence</T> show a short definition on hover. Click to open the [glossary](/glossary).
- **"Go deeper" boxes** hide the maths. Skip them on a first read.
- **Progress**: tick "Mark module as done" at the bottom of a module. It's saved in your browser only.
